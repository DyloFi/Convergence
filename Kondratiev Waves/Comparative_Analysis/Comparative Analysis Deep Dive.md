### 1.1 Schumpeterian Nested Cycles (Kitchin, Juglar, Kondratiev)
Joseph Schumpeter (1939) demonstrated that macroeconomic time series exhibit overlapping frequencies:
- **Kitchin (3–5 Yrs)**: Inventory fluctuations around business capacity.
- **Juglar (7–11 Yrs)**: Machine tool replacement and commercial bank credit expansion.
- **Kondratiev (45–60 Yrs)**: Basic infrastructure paradigm revolutions.

### 1.2 Long-Wave Interaction with Generational Saecula & Hegemonic Cycles
- **Strauss-Howe Generational Saeculum (~80–100 Yrs)**: Four 20-year generational turnings (High, Awakening, Unraveling, Crisis). K-Wave Winter resets systematically align with 4th Turning Crisis phases (Strauss & Howe, 1997).
- **Dalio Hegemonic Big Cycle (~150–250 Yrs)**: Rise, peak, and decline of imperial reserve currencies (Dutch Guilder -> British Pound -> US Dollar -> Multipolar Order). K-Wave A-phase expansion peaks historically trigger Great Power Hegemonic wars (Goldstein, 1988; Dalio, 2021).

## 3. Comparative Perspectives, Critiques & Counter-Theories (Comparative Analysis) (Comparative Analysis Deep Dive)

Comparative cycle modeling faces severe econometric critiques:

1. **Spectral Analysis & Filter Artifacts (Solomou, 1987; Kuznets, 1930)**: Solomou showed that moving-average detrending filters applied to macro series artificially generate 50-year periodicities (the Slutsky-Yule Effect). Spectral density analysis reveals that 15–25 year Kuznets swings possess stronger statistical support than deterministic 50-year Kondratiev waves.
2. **Unit Roots & Stochastic Random Walks (Nelson & Plosser, 1982)**: Macroeconomic time series possess unit roots $I(1)$ and follow stochastic trend processes:
$$Y_{t, kondratiev_wave} = \mu_{kondratiev_wave} + Y_{t-1, kondratiev_wave} + \epsilon_{t, kondratiev_wave}$$
   Cycles are not rigid harmonic oscillators, but stochastic random walks subject to permanent structural shocks.
3. **Neoclassical Growth Neutrality (Solow, 1956)**: Solow's model treats technical progress as smooth continuous exogenous growth $A(t) = A_0 e^{gt}$, rejecting multi-cycle taxonomies as arbitrary curve-fitting.

### Mathematical Multi-Cycle Harmonic Decomposition

Macroeconomic aggregate output $Y(t)$ can be formally represented as a superposed Fourier-Schumpeter harmonic series across four canonical frequencies:

$$Y(t)_{\text{comp}} = Y_0 e^{gt} + A_K \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{T_K} + \phi_K\right) + A_Z \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{T_Z} + \phi_Z\right) + A_J \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{T_J} + \phi_J\right) + A_C \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{T_C} + \phi_C\right)$$

where:
- $T_K \approx 54$ years (Kondratiev long wave)
- $T_Z \approx 18$ years (Kuznets infrastructure cycle)
- $T_J \approx 9$ years (Juglar capital cycle)
- $T_C \approx 3.5$ years (Kitchin inventory cycle)

When the phases of the three major cycles align at a local minimum (i.e., $\phi_K = \phi_Z = \phi_J = \frac{3\pi}{2}$), the system enters a systemic depression characterized by severe liquidity contraction and institutional dislocation:

$$\Delta Y_{\text{trough}} = -\left(A_K + A_Z + A_J + A_C\right)$$

Historical episodes of this destructive interference include 1873, 1929, 1973, and 2008.
