## Removed claims
1. **Deterministic Synchronous Climax (2026–2035)** as the central finding — unsupported (`F14-001`; RCM §2; Cross-Domain Matrix §2). Replaced with an evidence-weighted framework and an explicitly unscored scenario set.
2. **Repository AGI date (2026–2029) and ASI horizon (2032–2035)** — conflated external sources under different definitions; none were this repository's elicited forecasts (`F14-003`, `F14-004`, `F14-005`).
3. **Dated phase roadmap** (Section 5 of the old edition): YCC/fiscal dominance by 2026–2028, 40%+ enterprise workflow automation, AGI 2028–2031, sovereign nationalization of datacenters, ASI 2031–2035, post-dollar reserve framework by 2035 — no verified source supports these as dated predictions (`F14-009`, `F14-010`).
4. **"$500B+ annual semiconductor capex"** characterization — figure carried no verified citation.
5. **Implicit premise that capability, deployment, economic impact, and institutional effects form one trajectory** — the registers require these to stay distinct (`F14-002`, `F14-009`).
6. **"Sovereign debt monetization" as a current fact** — CBO documents baselines and rate sensitivities, not monetization (`F14-010`).
