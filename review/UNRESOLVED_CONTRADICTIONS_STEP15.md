# Unresolved Contradictions and Evidence Gaps — Step 15 (2026-11-08)

Recorded per DYL-48 deliverable requirements. These are disagreements the reconciled synthesis preserves rather than resolves.

## Contradictions between frameworks and data
1. **Fourth Turning timing vs. trust data.** The framework implies a Crisis-era civic re-mobilization in the 2008–2030s window, but Pew/NES show monotonic trust decline (77% → 17%, 1964–2025) with no crisis-era rebuilding (`CLM-S13-03`). Either the climax has not occurred, the framework lacks predictive content, or trust is the wrong indicator. Unresolved.
2. **Long-wave dating.** Sixth-wave onset estimates span 1971–2020 across Kondratiev, Schumpeter, Perez, Smihula, and Nefiodow (`CLM-S13-07`). Any claim about the current wave phase inherits this ±25-year ambiguity.
3. **Compute exponentialism vs. productivity stagnation.** Training compute grew nine orders of magnitude (2012–2026) while U.S. labor productivity decelerated to ~1.5%/yr post-2005 (`CLM-228` vs. `CLM-S13-13`; `F14-009`). Whether this is a lag (Scenario A) or a refutation of economic takeoff (Scenario C) is the central open question of the repository.
4. **AGI forecast incomparability.** LEAP (conditional expert median 2050) and Metaculus Q5121 (displayed 23 Sep 2030) use different definitions, conditionality, and aggregation (`F14-003`, `F14-004`). The ~20-year gap cannot be resolved by pooling; the repository tracks both under their own definitions.
5. **RWA metric ambiguity.** Distributed ($39.17B) and represented ($398.06B) RWA values differ by 10x; no independent reconciliation exists, and neither measures liquid settlement (`F14-006`).
6. **Reserve diversification vs. dollar resilience.** COFER decline and gold accumulation support gradual diversification (`CLM-001`, `F14-007`, `F14-008`), while the absence of a successor reserve asset and continued SWIFT/CHIPS dominance support dollar persistence. Both are documented; the trajectory rate is the unresolved variable.
7. **Geopolitical acceleration vs. regulation.** U.S.–China rivalry pressures toward maximal AI scaling (§2.5), while defensive regulation and governance negotiations push the other way. Which dominates through 2035 is undecided.

## Evidence gaps
1. **No pre-registered convergence scoring rule** (`F14-001`). Until domains, thresholds, lags, and a joint-event rule are registered, the composite thesis can neither be confirmed nor falsified as a single event.
2. **No empirical estimate of the recursive self-improvement coefficient γ.** The takeoff equation's divergence regime (γ > 1) is untested; METR-style capability series are the closest proxy and carry an explicit measurement ceiling (`F14-002`).
3. **No causal attribution method linking AI capability to aggregate productivity.** BLS series do not decompose AI contributions (`F14-009`); sector-level natural experiments are missing from the repository.
4. **Metaculus Q5121 resolution criteria unverified** in the extracted page text (`F14-004`); the displayed estimate is usable only as a point-in-time reading.
5. **2026 gold and productivity series are incomplete-year** (`F14-008`; next BLS preliminary release was scheduled 2026-11-05, after the Step 14 review date of 2026-11-01).
6. **Institutional-trust counter-trend data**: watermarking/provenance adoption (e.g., C2PA) is asserted as a counterforce in the cross-domain matrix but has no adoption time series in the repository.
7. **Legacy confidence percentages.** Older review documents (e.g., `CROSS_DOMAIN_MATRIX.md`) carry numeric confidence levels (42%–85%) with no elicitation record; per the Step 14 audit they are retained as historical artifacts, not calibrated estimates, and the rebuilt synthesis uses qualitative labels instead.

## Disposition
Each item is either tracked in the scenario indicators (synthesis §5) or in the falsification framework (§7). None blocks the rebuilt synthesis; they bound what it claims.
