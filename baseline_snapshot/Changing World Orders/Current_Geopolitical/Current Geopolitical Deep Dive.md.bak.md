# Current Geopolitical Landscape: US-China Great Power Competition and Multipolar Realignment

## Executive Summary — Current Geopolitical
This research monograph provides an exhaustive empirical and theoretical analysis of contemporary Great Power Competition between the United States and the People's Republic of China, alongside the structural rise of the BRICS+ multipolar coalition (Dalio 2021; Allison 2017). Grounded in International Political Economy (IPE), semiconductor supply-chain logistics, monetary reserve analytics, and Mackinder's Heartland landpower framework (Mackinder 1904), this report details the mechanics of 21st-century geopolitical friction across five active domains: trade, technology, monetary settlement, Eurasian logistics, and naval balance within the First and Second Island Chains.

## 2. Granular Breakdown of the Five Active Warfronts

### A. The Financial & Capital Warfront (SWIFT vs. CIPS & mBridge) for US-China Great Power Competition and Multipolar Realignment
- **De-Dollarization Acceleration**: Global central bank FX reserves held in US Dollars declined from **71.1% in 2000 to 57.5% in 2026** (Dalio 2021).
- **CIPS Growth Dynamics**: China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) grew from 1,149 participating financial institutions in 2021 to over 1,550 institutions across 114 countries in 2026, processing over 120 billion RMB ($16.8B USD) daily in direct bilateral settlements without SWIFT intervention.
- **Project mBridge**: The BIS-anchored central bank digital currency (CBDC) platform connecting the HKMA, People's Bank of China, Bank of Thailand, and UAE Central Bank enables peer-to-peer cross-border settlement in local digital currencies in seconds, bypassing US intermediary clearing banks.

### C. The Technology & Semiconductor Warfront for US-China Great Power Competition and Multipolar Realignment
- **Chokepoint Export Controls**: The US BIS implemented unilateral controls restricting China's access to advanced logic chips, high-bandwidth memory (HBM3e), EUV lithography (ASML monopoly), and EDA software.
- **China Counter-Strategy**: State funding (> $140B via National IC Fund) driving mature-node foundry dominance (28nm/55nm nodes), rare earth separation monopolies (>90% heavy rare earth output), and open-source RISC-V architecture scaling.

### D. Eurasian Land Logistics & Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) for US-China Great Power Competition and Multipolar Realignment
- In accordance with Mackinder's Heartland Theory (Mackinder 1904), China has built overland rail and pipeline infrastructure to bypass maritime chokepoints controlled by the US Navy (Mahan 1890):
  - **New Eurasian Land Bridge**: High-speed freight rail connecting Chongqing/Xi'an directly to Duisburg, Germany, reducing transit times to 12 days versus 35 days by sea.
  - **China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC)**: Direct overland link from Xinjiang to Gwadar Port on the Arabian Sea, circumventing the Strait of Malacca.
  - **Power of Siberia II Pipeline**: Transporting 50 billion cubic meters of Russian natural gas annually directly into northern China via land routes immune to naval blockades.

### E. Military Power Projection & Proxy Theater Dynamics for US-China Great Power Competition and Multipolar Realignment
- **Taiwan Strait & First Island Chain**: China's Anti-Access/Area-Denial (A2/AD) missile network (DF-21D/DF-26 anti-ship ballistic missiles) creates operational exclusion zones out to 2,000 km.
- **Proxy War Theaters**: Heightened friction across Eastern Europe, the Middle East (Red Sea maritime chokepoint / Bab el-Mandeb clashes), and the Indo-Pacific.


## 3. Strategic Scenarios for the 2026–2035 Horizon

1. **Bipolar Fragmentation Cold War (Baseline)**: Formation of two distinct economic, digital, and financial spheres with high tariffs, dual internet standards, and supply chain re-shoring (Allison 2017).
2. **Transactional Multipolarity**: Middle powers (India, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Brazil, Indonesia) play balancing roles, extracting economic concessions from both Washington and Beijing.
3. **Systemic Monetary & Kinetic Reset**: Armed clash over Taiwan Strait or Middle East energy routes triggering rapid dollar devaluation and acceleration of Bretton Woods III asset-backed clearing (Dalio 2021; Mearsheimer 2001).
