## 2. Theoretical Mechanics of Cross-Cycle Resonance (Supporting Materials)

### A. The 250-Year Hegemonic Cycle vs. 80-Year Saeculum
A 250-year Big Cycle contains approximately three 80-year Strauss-Howe saeculums (Strauss & Howe 1997; Dalio 2021):
- **Saeculum 1 (Rise Phase) (Supporting Materials Deep Dive & Cross-Domain Synthesis)**: Institutional building following global conflict climax (e.g., US Post-Revolutionary / Early Republic era 1780–1860).
- **Saeculum 2 (Peak Phase) (Supporting Materials Deep Dive & Cross-Domain Synthesis)**: Industrial scaling, commercial expansion, and international empire building (e.g., US Gilded Age to Post-WWII Pax Americana 1865–1945).
- **Saeculum 3 (Decline & Crisis Phase) (Supporting Materials Deep Dive & Cross-Domain Synthesis)**: Financial overextension, debt monetization, wealth polarization, and Fourth Turning crisis climax (e.g., 1945–2026+).

### B. The 50-Year K-Wave Engine of Imperial Competitiveness
Each 250-year hegemonic era is fueled by five sequential 50-year Kondratiev innovation waves (Kondratiev 1925; Perez 2002):
1. **K1 (1780s–1840s)**: Steam engine, iron smelting, cotton textiles (British Rise).
2. **K2 (1840s–1890s)**: Railways, Bessemer steel, steamships (British Peak).
3. **K3 (1890s–1940s)**: Heavy engineering, electrical power, chemistry, internal combustion (US Rise).
4. **K4 (1940s–1990s)**: Petrochemicals, mass production, aviation, nuclear power (US Peak).
5. **K5 (1990s–2020s)**: Microelectronics, personal computing, software, Internet (US Financialization Autumn).
6. **K6 (2020s–2070s)**: Artificial Intelligence, robotics, quantum compute, synthetic biology (Multipolar Competition Phase).
