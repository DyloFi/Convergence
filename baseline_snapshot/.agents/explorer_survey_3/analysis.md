## 4. R2 Report Enhancement Guidelines & Implementation Protocol

Round 2 (R2) implementers will systematically incorporate the recommendations from the 5 Expert Review reports directly into all 143 subfolder markdown reports. To ensure rigorous academic quality, structural consistency, and total compliance with automated verification scripts, implementers MUST follow these mandatory guidelines:

### Guideline 1 — Substantive Line Count Standard (>50 Lines Threshold)
- **Requirement**: Every report MUST strictly exceed 50 lines of code (`line_count > 50`). Target line count for expanded reports is **100 to 250 lines**.
- **Boilerplate Prohibition**: Implementers MUST remove generic, copy-pasted "Academic Appendix" blocks and replace them with **custom, domain-specific substantive research**.
- **No Blank Padding**: Line counts must reflect genuine academic content (tables, derivations, paragraphs, citations), not excessive whitespace or empty markdown headers.

### Guideline 2 — Formal In-Text Academic Citations & References
- **Requirement**: Every report MUST contain formal in-text academic citations (e.g., `(Strauss & Howe, 1997)`, `(Perez, 2002)`, `(Bostrom, 2014)`) and end with a dedicated `## Academic References & Bibliography` section.
- **Minimum References**: At least **3 to 5 formal academic references** (books, journal papers, conference proceedings) per subfolder report.

### Guideline 3
- **Requirement**: Reports MUST include quantitative empirical data points formatted in Markdown tables or explicit mathematical notation.
- **Subject Datasets**:
  - `4th Turning`: Demographic cohort sizes, Pew trust percentages, saecular war casualties, debt ratios.
  - `Changing World Orders`: Reserve currency global share %, debt-to-GDP ratios, military budget comparisons, historical trade volume data.
  - `Kondratiev Waves`: Commodity price indices (1790–2026), real interest rates, long-wave peak/trough years, patent frequency data.
  - `LoAR`: Computations per $1,000 time-series, transistor counts, lithography node sizes (μm to nm), DNA sequencing cost per megabase ($), FLOP scaling numbers.
  - `Technological Singularity`: Model parameters, training FLOPs ($10^{20}$ to $10^{26}$), horizon probability distribution estimates, compute hardware efficiency metrics.

### Guideline 4 — Structural Standardization & Section Architecture
Every expanded report MUST follow a clean, standardized Markdown architecture tailored to its domain:

```markdown
# [Report Title]: [Sub-Topic Focus]

## Executive Summary
[High-level summary of findings, scope, and strategic relevance]

## 1. Theoretical Framework & Core Principles
[In-depth theoretical foundation with formal in-text citations]

## 2. Quantitative Data, Empirical Metrics & Historical Case Studies
[Markdown tables, time-series data, mathematical derivations, historical evidence]

## 3. Comparative Perspectives, Critiques & Counter-Theories
[Evaluation of counter-arguments, methodological limitations, alternative interpretations]

## 4. Strategic Implications & Future Trajectories (2026–2035+)
[Actionable policy, financial, technological, or institutional takeaways]

## Academic References & Bibliography
[Formal citations in APA/Chicago style]
```

### Guideline 5
- **Requirement**: Reports should cross-reference relevant touchpoints from the other 4 civilizational super-cycle dimensions (e.g., how a Kondratiev Wave winter aligns with a 4th Turning Crisis or Hegemonic Decline) in a **customized, context-aware paragraph** rather than a generic pasted graphic.
