# Forecasting Scorecards and Calibration Track Records: AGI Consensus Timelines and Macro-Forecaster Audits

## Executive Summary
This empirical research monograph establishes a rigorous, quantitative calibration scorecard evaluating past and present forecasting track records across macro-historical cycles and technological horizons. Forecasting complex, non-linear evolutionary transitions is notoriously susceptible to cognitive biases, narrative fallacies, and retrospective cherry-picking. To overcome these limitations, this monograph audits two complementary forecasting domains: (1) forward-looking artificial general intelligence (AGI) consensus distributions, tracking the longitudinal compression of arrival timelines on Metaculus, prediction markets (Polymarket, Manifold), and expert researcher panels from AI Impacts (Grace et al., 2018, 2022, 2024); and (2) forensic, retrospective calibration scorecards evaluating historical predictions made by seminal cycle and technological forecasters, specifically Ray Kurzweil (*The Age of Spiritual Machines*, 1999; *The Singularity is Near*, 2005), William Strauss and Neil Howe (*Generations*, 1991; *The Fourth Turning*, 1997), and foundational Kondratiev long-wave economists (Schumpeter, 1939; Mensch, 1979; Perez, 2002). By evaluating forecasted dates, confidence intervals, and empirical real-world outcomes, this report establishes a quantitative baseline for scoring the Convergence thesis through the 2026–2035 window.

### Post-HLMI Takeoff Dynamics: Fast vs Slow Takeoff Probability
Grace et al. (2024) specifically polled researchers on the probability that an AI system would become "vastly better than humans in all tasks" within two years of achieving High-Level Machine Intelligence (the classical Vinge-Good-Bostrom Intelligence Explosion):

- **Thirty Years Post-HLMI**: Median probability assigned to an intelligence explosion reached **60% to 80%**.

The survey confirms that even among academic machine learning researchers historically skeptical of singularitarian claims, the projected arrival date for transformative AI has experienced dramatic, unprecedented forward acceleration.

## 3. Forensic Calibration Scorecard: Ray Kurzweil (1999–2026)

Ray Kurzweil remains the most prominent quantitative technological forecaster of the exponential paradigm. In *The Age of Spiritual Machines* (1999) and *The Singularity is Near* (2005), Kurzweil published over 140 specific forecasts with explicit target dates (1999, 2009, 2019, 2029, and 2045). 

While Kurzweil himself claimed an 86% accuracy rate (Kurzweil, 2010), independent retrospective audits by Nick Bostrom, Luke Muehlhauser, and Stuart Armstrong (Armstrong & Sotala, 2015) applied strict calibration criteria, categorizing forecasts as True, Partially True, False, or Premature.

### Kurzweil Calibration Synthesis
- **Strict Independent Accuracy**: Independent evaluations score Kurzweil's 2009 and 2019 predictions at **62% to 68% strictly correct**, with an additional **15% partially correct or delayed by 3 to 5 years** (Armstrong & Sotala, 2015).
- **Core Vector Accuracy**: Kurzweil was exceptionally accurate regarding computational price-performance, wireless network diffusion, and voice synthesis. His primary bias was an **underestimation of regulatory, commercial, and physical integration friction** (e.g., consumer adoption of smart glasses and fully driverless vehicle legal certification).
- **The 2029 AGI Forecast**: Formulated in 1999 when consensus predicted AGI centuries away, Kurzweil's unwavering prediction of AGI by 2029 has transitioned from extreme heterodoxy into mainstream scientific consensus.

### Epistemic Assessment of Generational Theory
While Strauss and Howe provide a compelling cyclical narrative, rigorous calibration requires noting significant epistemic vulnerabilities:
- **Retrospective Narrative Fitting**: Generational boundaries and turning catalysts are susceptible to selection bias, where historical milestones are retroactively designated as turning boundaries to preserve the ~20-year saecular periodicity.
- **Subjective Archetypal Reduction**: Attributing homogeneous socio-psychological traits (Prophet, Nomad, Hero, Artist) to entire demographic generations risks oversimplification and obscures significant socio-economic, racial, and geographic heterogeneity.

### Epistemic Assessment of Long-Wave Economics
Applying Joseph Schumpeter's 1939 treatise *Business Cycles* to early 21st-century software and artificial intelligence is a retrospective theoretical synthesis developed by neo-Schumpeterian scholars (Perez, 2002; Freeman & Louçã, 2001), not an empirical forecast made by Schumpeter himself. Furthermore, while Carlota Perez's model of installation, frenzy, turning point, and deployment provides an exceptionally lucid descriptive taxonomy of tech capital cycles, it is a historical heuristic rather than a predictive econometric instrument; the exact boundaries between 'frenzy' and 'synergy' can rarely be identified in real time.

## 7. Falsification & Disconfirmation Framework for Technological Forecasting

To maintain rigorous epistemic hygiene, forecasting models evaluating AGI arrival horizons and technological singularity dynamics must specify unambiguous empirical invalidation criteria:

1. **Metaculus and Market Timeline Expansion**:
   - The Metaculus community median date for AGI expanding past 2045, accompanied by prediction markets pricing <10% probability of human-level AI prior to 2040.
   - Long-term stabilization of AI Impacts researcher surveys where the median estimated arrival date for High-Level Machine Intelligence (HLMI) extends beyond 2060.
2. **Benchmark Capability Saturation and Task Failure**:
   - Frontier AI models demonstrating persistent asymptotic saturation on verified coding, formal mathematics, and autonomous multi-hour planning suites (SWE-bench Verified ceiling below 85%, ARC-AGI below 85%, METR task horizons failing to exceed 24 hours with >50% reliability through 2028).
   - Inability of recursive self-improvement and test-time search scaling to generate novel, unassisted mathematical proofs or automated hardware designs beyond human specialist capabilities.
3. **Generational Cycle Disconfirmation**:
   - Resolution of contemporary civic, partisan, and institutional tensions without structural institutional restructuring, accompanied by a durable re-establishment of institutional trust across all major demographic cohorts.
4. **Long-Wave Technological Decoupling**:
   - Massive retraction in global AI datacenter CapEx accompanied by hyperscaler write-downs and the failure of AI infrastructure to create viable commercial software and hardware ecosystems (resembling the AI winters of 1974 and 1987).

If these empirical markers occur, the compressed timeline and Convergence hypothesis must be formally rejected.
