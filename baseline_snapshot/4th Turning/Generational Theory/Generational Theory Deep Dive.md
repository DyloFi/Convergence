## 1. Theoretical Framework & Generational Mechanics (Generational Theory)
Generational theory asserts that social history is driven by the dynamic interaction of four distinct age cohorts moving through life.
Because each generation reacts against the perceived excesses of its elders, the saeculum maintains a self-correcting equilibrium.

### 1.1 Archetype Dynamics in Comprehensive Theoretical Mechanics of Generational Turnings
- **Prophet Generation (Idealist)**: Moralistic idealists who champion spiritual values in youth and provide uncompromising moral authority during Crisis climaxes.
- **Nomad Generation (Reactive)**: Alienated reactives who cultivate tough survival skills in youth and execute pragmatic operational leadership during Crises.
- **Hero Generation (Civic)**: Protected civics who build powerful youth cadres during Crises and steward industrial/institutional expansion in midlife.
- **Artist Generation (Adaptive)**: Sensitive adaptives who seek consensus during Highs and manage complex administrative networks in mature adulthood.

### 1.2 Saecular Phase Mapping
1. **First Turning (High)**: Society rebuilds civic infrastructure under the unified leadership of mature Heroes and elder Artists.
2. **Second Turning (Awakening)**: Young Prophets ignite passionate cultural and moral revolts against the triumph of High institutional order.
3. **Third Turning (Unraveling)**: Midlife Prophets and young Nomads steward a culture of hyper-individualism, causing institutional authority to disintegrate.
4. **Fourth Turning (Crisis)**: Elder Prophets guide, midlife Nomads execute, and young Heroes fight through existential peril to forge a new order.
