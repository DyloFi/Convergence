## 1. Theoretical Framework & Generational Mechanics (Concept)
The saeculum represents a complete human lifespan, approximately 80 to 100 years, divided into four distinct 20-22 year turnings.
Each turning shifts the public mood, institutional trust, and social cohesion as generational cohorts move into new life stages.

### 1.1 Archetype Dynamics in Theoretical Foundations of the Saecular Turnings Concept
- **Prophet Generation (Idealist)**: Idealist cohorts born during First Turnings whose moral passions catalyze Second Turning Awakenings and structure Fourth Turning ideological battles.
- **Nomad Generation (Reactive)**: Reactive cohorts born during Second Turnings whose cynical self-reliance maintains operational sanity during Third Turning fragmentation.
- **Hero Generation (Civic)**: Civic cohorts born during Third Turnings whose collective discipline provides the physical manpower for Fourth Turning institutional rebuilds.
- **Artist Generation (Adaptive)**: Adaptive cohorts born during Fourth Turnings whose cautious expertise manages bureaucratic stability in post-crisis Highs.

### 1.2 Saecular Phase Mapping
1. **First Turning (High)**: Institutions expand and consolidate; public mood is confident and collective; individualism is subdued in favor of civic consensus.
2. **Second Turning (Awakening)**: Institutions are challenged in the name of personal expression; spiritual and cultural passions ignite; civic consensus weakens.
3. **Third Turning (Unraveling)**: Institutions decay while individual autonomy peaks; public trust fragments; political polarization and culture wars intensify.
4. **Fourth Turning (Crisis)**: Institutions are torn down and rebuilt under threat of civilizational collapse; public peril demands total civic mobilization.
