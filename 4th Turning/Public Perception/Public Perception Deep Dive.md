## 1. Theoretical Framework & Generational Mechanics — Public Sentiment, Civic Mood & Polling Trends
Public sentiment functions as the emotional barometer of the saeculum, reflecting the collective mood of living generations.
Tracking longitudinal polling data confirms that public mood changes follow predictable cyclical phase progressions.

### 1.1 Archetype Dynamics in Public Sentiment, Civic Mood & Polling Trends
- **Prophet Generation (Idealist)**: Drives moral polarization in public sentiment polling, expressing low compromise tolerance and high values-based anger.
- **Nomad Generation (Reactive)**: Displays consistent cynicism in polling toward government institutions, preferring self-reliant private solutions.
- **Hero Generation (Civic)**: Shows rising demand for strong leadership, high community engagement, and willingness to support collective national projects.
- **Artist Generation (Adaptive)**: Exhibits high desire for social harmony, high anxiety metrics in surveys, and strong preference for expert consensus.

### 1.2 Saecular Phase Mapping for Public Sentiment, Civic Mood & Polling Trends
1. **First Turning (High)**: Polling shows 75%+ public trust in federal institutions, high national pride, and strong confidence in the future.
2. **Second Turning (Awakening)**: Polling registers surging cultural controversy, declining church attendance, and rising anti-establishment sentiment.
3. **Third Turning (Unraveling)**: Polling captures trust dropping to sub-30% levels, high political alienation, and intense partisan animosity.
4. **Fourth Turning (Crisis)**: Polling records sharp V-shaped rebound in demand for state intervention, collective security, and national unity.

### Mathematical Modeling of Public Trust Collapse Dynamics

The erosion of institutional trust can be modeled as a non-linear tipping process. Let $\mathcal{T}(t) \in [0, 1]$ represent public trust. The decay rate accelerates as perceived institutional performance $\mathcal{Q}(t)$ falls below expectations:

$$\frac{d \mathcal{T}}{dt} = -\alpha \mathcal{T}(1 - \mathcal{T}) + \beta (\mathcal{Q}(t) - \mathcal{Q}_0) - \mu \mathcal{P}_{\text{scandal}}(t)$$

Once trust drops below a critical threshold $\mathcal{T}^* \approx 0.30$, institutional actions designed to restore credibility are paradoxically interpreted as cynical manipulation, accelerating the transition into Fourth Turning emergency demand.
