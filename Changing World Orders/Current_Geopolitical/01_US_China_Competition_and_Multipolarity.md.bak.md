# US-China Competition and the Multipolar World Order

## Executive Summary — 01 US China Competition and Multipolarity
The contemporary geopolitical landscape is defined by the structural transition from a post-Cold War unipolar order (dominated by the United States) to a contested, highly volatile multipolar order (Dalio 2021). The primary systemic fault line lies between the incumbent hegemon (the US and its allied G7/NATO network) and a rising challenger coalition (led by China and Russia, anchored by BRICS+ expansion and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization) (Allison 2017; Mearsheimer 2001). Grounded in Hegemonic Stability Theory (Gilpin 1981) and Power Transition Theory (Organski & Kugler 1980), this report evaluates the conflict across five synchronized warfronts: financial/monetary, technological/semiconductor, geopolitical alignment, resource/supply chain, and naval/military power.

### Table 1: The Four Asymmetric Warfronts of Hegemonic Competition

| Warfront Category | Primary Conflict Vector | Strategic Offensive Mechanisms | Contested Frontlines / Theater |
|---|---|---|---|

### A. Financial & Monetary Warfront for US-China Competition and the Multipolar World Order
- **US Financial Hegemony**: Maintenance of the US Dollar as global reserve currency (~57.5% of central bank reserves) and SWIFT network settlement dominance (~85% of foreign exchange transactions) (Dalio 2021).
- **Challenger De-Dollarization**: Expansion of China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS), bilateral local-currency trade settlement (RMB/Ruble/Rupee), central bank gold accumulation, and mBridge multi-CBDC trial networks.

### B. Technology & Semiconductor Warfront for US-China Competition and the Multipolar World Order
- **US Containment Policy**: Implementation of BIS export controls restricting advanced AI accelerators (NVIDIA/AMD), extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography tools (ASML), and Electronic Design Automation (EDA) software.
- **China Industrial Subsidies**: Allocation of over $140B in state capital (Big Fund Phase I–III) toward indigenous wafer fabrication, domestic DUV/EUV tool development, and AI compute acceleration.

### C. Geopolitical Alignment & Global South Coalition for US-China Competition and the Multipolar World Order
- **Western System**: NATO transatlantic expansion, G7 economic coordination, AUKUS maritime technology sharing, and the Quad security dialogue.
- **Eurasian / BRICS+ Alignment**: Expansion of BRICS (incorporating Saudi Arabia, UAE, Iran, Ethiopia, Egypt) representing >37% of global GDP (PPP) and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO).

## 5. Future Scenarios for the Multipolar World Order (2026–2035)

1. **Managed Multipolar Alignment (Bretton Woods III)**: High-level bilateral accords establish guardrails around AI, cyber warfare, and monetary clearing, preserving trade integration while allowing currency basket diversification (Dalio 2021).
2. **Fragmented Economic Cold War (Baseline)**: Dual technology and monetary ecosystems emerge (US-centric vs China-centric), enforcing tariff walls, supply chain re-shoring, and proxy warfare in peripheral states (Allison 2017).
3. **Systemic Monetary & Conflict Shock**: Rapid debt monetization forces dollar devaluation, prompting weaponization of trade routes and kinetic confrontation over maritime chokepoints (Mearsheimer 2001; Gilpin 1981).
