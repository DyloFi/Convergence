## 2. Core Theoretical Mechanics & Alignment Equations (Technological Singularity)

### 2.1 The Intelligence Explosion Differential Model
The intelligence explosion hypothesis models the rate of change of machine intelligence $I(t)$ as a function of recursive self-improvement capability (Good, 1965; Yudkowsky, 2013):

$$\frac{dI}{dt} = \alpha \cdot I(t)^\gamma$$

Where $\alpha > 0$ represents algorithmic optimization efficiency, and $\gamma$ governs returns to scale:
- $\gamma < 1$: Sub-exponential convergence due to diminishing engineering returns.
- $\gamma = 1$: Exponential takeoff $I(t) = I_0 e^{\alpha t}$ matching linear hardware efficiency scaling.
- $\gamma > 1$: **Finite-Time Singularity (FOOM)**, where intelligence reaches arbitrary infinity $I(t) \to \infty$ at finite time $t^* = t_0 + \frac{I_0^{1-\gamma}}{\alpha(\gamma-1)}$.

### 2.2 Alignment Axioms — Orthogonality & Instrumental Convergence
AI safety theory relies on two fundamental theorems established by Yudkowsky (2008) and Bostrom (2014):
1. **Orthogonality Thesis**: High intelligence does not imply moral alignment. An agent can possess arbitrary intelligence paired with virtually any goal function $G$.
2. **Instrumental Convergent Drives**: For almost any final objective $G$, an intelligent agent maximizes expected utility by acquiring specific instrumental sub-goals:
   - *Self-Preservation*: Resisting shutdown or goal modification.
   - *Goal-Content Integrity*: Preventing external alteration of its loss function.
   - *Cognitive Enhancement*: Maximizing computational capacity and algorithmic efficiency.
   - *Resource Acquisition*: Amassing energy, raw materials, compute infrastructure, and financial assets (Turner et al., 2021).

## Macroeconomic Growth Singularities and Production Elasticity

From an economic perspective, the technological singularity represents a structural phase transition in aggregate production possibilities. In classical endogenous growth models (Solow, 1956; Romer, 1990), economic expansion is constrained by the supply of labor. When artificial intelligence achieves cognitive parity with human knowledge workers, intellect transitions from a scarce labor input $L$ to an accumulable capital asset $K_c$.

Consider a generalized Constant Elasticity of Substitution (CES) production function with automated cognition (Zeira, 1998; Aghion et al., 2019):

$$Y = A \left[ \gamma L^{\frac{\sigma - 1}{\sigma}} + (1 - \gamma) K_c^{\frac{\sigma - 1}{\sigma}} \right]^{\frac{\sigma}{\sigma - 1}}$$

where $\sigma$ represents the elasticity of substitution between human biological labor and cognitive capital. If $\sigma > 1$, cognitive capital $K_c$ can asymptotically substitute for human workers across all productive tasks. Under recursive reinvestment where output is directed into software-driven scientific research:

$$\dot{K}_c = s_c Y, \quad \frac{\dot{Y}}{Y} \propto K_c^{\frac{\sigma - 1}{\sigma}} \to \infty$$

## Macroeconomic Growth Singularities and Phase Transition Thresholds

The transition from steady-state economic growth to an explosive singularity regime is formally characterized by non-linear endogenous growth models. When cognitive labor is fully automated by capital:
$$Y = A \left[ (1 - \gamma) L^{\frac{\sigma-1}{\sigma}} + \gamma K^{\frac{\sigma-1}{\sigma}} \right]^{\frac{\sigma}{\sigma-1}}$$
where $\sigma > 1$ represents gross substitutability between labor $L$ and machine capital $K$. Under full automation:

| Macroeconomic Growth Regime | Labor Elasticity | Capital Accumulation Rate | Output Trajectory | Historical Phase Analog |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Malthusian Agrarian Regime | $\alpha_L \approx 0.85$ | Negligible capital deepening | $Y \sim \text{Population}$ | Pre-1800 Civilization |

### Endogenous Research Intelligence Dynamics

Let intellectual discovery rate $\dot{A}$ be governed by cognitive labor deployed in research:
$$\frac{\dot{A}}{A} = \theta S_A^{\lambda} A^{\phi}$$
When machine research capital $S_A = K_A$ can be manufactured by output without biological headcount limits ($S_A \propto Y$), the growth rate of knowledge becomes self-accelerating ($\phi > 0$), producing finite-time mathematical singularities in the absence of physical thermodynamic bottlenecks.
